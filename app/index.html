Roadmap to Platinum

  1. Step 1 — Story + optional missions ~60–80 hours. Play the story; do the Chapter 1 meditation; take optional missions as they appear (100% needs any 100 of several hundred); scan every new vehicle with WAiNK (100% needs 200 of 277); study animals through binoculars (100% needs 100 of 255). Story 0 of 12 earned
  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist ~100 hours. The checklist appears after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 card sets, 15 of 18 meditations, 300 of 397 stunts, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 100 optional missions, all 39 illegal operations, all 8 bank vaults, Bret's 15 target decks. Checklist trophies 0 of 28 earned
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks + cleanup ~10 hours. Eight stranger strands — not part of 100% — then clean up whatever is left. Platinum follows. Total: ~200 hours (PowerPyx), 250–300 hours (Push Square). Difficulty 4/10. Cheats disable trophies. Strangers 0 of 7 earned

Missables

Do this first: Positive Mental Attitude. The only trophy any source flags as possibly missable. One Chapter 1 morning meditation at Jason's house — Lucia meditates on the balcony; follow her and accept the optional quest — is gone after Chapter 1. It is unclear whether the trophy needs that one spot, since it is not marked like the other 18. PowerPyx flags it; Push Square lists no missables. All 12 story trophies are unmissable, Kenton returns later, and the Kingpin Bowling robbery returns later.
