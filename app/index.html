Missables

DO THIS FIRST — possibly missable

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots). One morning meditation in Chapter 1, at Jason's house, may be gone after Chapter 1: Lucia meditates on the balcony, so follow her and accept the optional quest. PowerPyx flags it; Push Square lists no missables; it is not confirmed whether the trophy needs that one morning. Every other trophy is confirmed unmissable.

Roadmap to Platinum

  1. Step 1 — Story & optional missions · 60–80 hours. Play the story; take optional missions as they appear; do the Chapter 1 meditation the first morning it is offered; scan every new vehicle with WAiNK; study animals through binoculars. Story trophies 0/12
  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist · about 100 hours. The checklist appears after the story under Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. It needs 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of 18 card sets, 15 of 18 meditations, 300 of 397 stunts, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 100 optional missions, all 39 illegal operations, all 8 bank vaults, and Bret's 15 target decks. 100% trophies 0/3
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup · about 10 hours. Finish the 8 stranger strands — they are not part of the 100% checklist — then sweep up anything left. The platinum follows. Stranger strands 0/7
